Hello, I am Kuter. I am currently a research intern at the [Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy](https://www.mpi-sp.org/) with [Jana Hofmann](https://janahofmann.github.io/) I will soon be starting as a PhD student at MPI-SP with [Hugo Lefeuvre](https://owl.eu.com/).

My research lies at the intersection of formal methods and systems. This means that I formalize systems and show that they actually do what they are intended to do, using tools such as ACL2, Rocq, and Verus.
